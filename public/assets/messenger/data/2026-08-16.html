Welcome Back
“Are you ready for the new school year?”

If I had received a dollar every time I have been asked that question, I would be rich by now. My answer has always been, “No.” Some people laughed when I said it. Others probably thought I wasn’t willing to go back, or that I hadn’t done my due diligence to prepare.

None of that is what I mean when I say that I am not ready. What I mean is something much simpler and, I think, much truer: you cannot be ready for what you don’t know is coming your way.

The truth is that we have no idea what will happen tomorrow, let alone during an entire school year. We have predictions and expectations based on our plans, and we hope that everything will unfold according to those plans. For that, we might be ready.

But the world, life, and God Himself do not bend to our plans. It is the other way around: our plans must ultimately bend to reality, and reality is held in God’s providence.

I do agree with the principle, “Failing to plan is planning to fail.” But I think it needs a second part: even if you plan thoroughly, you can still fail. Not necessarily because your planning was poor, but because life is not entirely in our hands.

All I can truly be ready for is to give my best; to adapt when God changes my plans; to accept His will and its outcome without complaining; to surrender all my efforts and work to Him; and to do my due diligence to serve Him and those entrusted to me as best I can.

So perhaps the real question is not, “Are you ready for the new school year?”

Perhaps the real question is: Are you ready to let God be God in your life? Are you ready to let Him lead your projects and shape your plans?

If the answer is yes, then you are ready.

In Mary, Christ; through Christ, to the Father!

Fr. James de Cendra, dcjm
