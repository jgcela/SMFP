Volunteer at St. Mary

Give your time and talent, from classroom support to school events. Start with the three steps below, then pick an opportunity.

Before you volunteer

Three steps for every volunteer

  1. 1

    Complete VIRTUS training

    Register for a VIRTUS account and complete the training. This comes first, before you volunteer in any role.

    How to register (PDF)

  2. 2

    Request your background check

    Once you're VIRTUS trained, email the front office. We'll send the form directly to your email, so your Social Security number is never shared with anyone else.

    Email the front office

  3. 3

    Log your service hours in FACTS

    1. Log in to FACTS Family Portal and go to Family › Family Home.
    2. Choose the person from the menu at the top of the page.
    3. Select Add+ in the Service Hours section.
    4. Enter the date, hours and a description, then select Save.

    Step-by-step guide (PDF)
